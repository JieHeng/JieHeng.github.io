Sudoku Privacy Policy

Effective 4 October 2026

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app does not request internet access, does not ask for any device permissions, and contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services.

Information stored on your device

To let you pick up where you left off, the app saves the following on your device only:

  • your current game in progress (the board, your notes, the timer, mistakes and hints used)
  • your settings, such as theme, difficulty and sound preferences

This information never leaves your device through the app, and we cannot access it. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data in your device settings.

Device backups

If you have turned on Android's backup feature, Android may include the app's saved game and settings in your device backup, which is stored in your Google account. This backup is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. We have no access to it.

App updates

The app uses Google Play's in-app update feature to let you know when a new version is available. This check is performed by the Google Play Store app on your device and is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. No information is sent to us.

Children's privacy

The app is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects no personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
